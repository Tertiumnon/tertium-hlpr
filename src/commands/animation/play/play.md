# animation play

Play a terminal animation until you press `q` or `Esc` (Ctrl+C also exits).

## Usage

```bash
hlpr animation play <wave|stars|fire|pipes|matrix|random>
```

`random` picks one of the animations above at random.

- `wave` - a rolling sine wave
- `stars` - a starfield flying toward you
- `fire` - rising flames
- `pipes` - the Windows 98 3D Pipes screensaver, drawn flat with colored pipes that turn and grow until the screen clears
- `matrix` - the same Wake Up Neo sequence and Matrix rain as `hlpr hello`

Requires an interactive terminal. Works on Windows, Linux, and macOS.
